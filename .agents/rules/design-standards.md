# Design & Engineering Standards: Taste + UI/UX Pro Max

This workspace enforces elite design taste and UI/UX engineering standards:

1. **Aesthetics & Taste**:
   - Follow Apple Design Guidelines (dark obsidian themes, translucent frosted glass, specular highlights).
   - Reject generic AI templates, unnatural purple gradients, and harsh grey borders.
   - Maintain typographic hierarchy with optical tracking and generous whitespace.

2. **UX & Ergonomics**:
   - Touch targets >= 44x44px.
   - Thumb-zone placement for mobile actions with iOS safe area padding.
   - 7-state interaction model (Default, Hover, Active, Focus, Disabled, Loading, Success/Error).
   - Humanized, jargon-free copy for everyday users.

3. **Performance**:
   - 60/120fps GPU animations (`transform`, `opacity` only).
   - Use Apple curve `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
